Course Objectives
COObjective
CO1Identify and recall fundamental concepts and techniques for designing intelligent agents.
CO2Represent and use knowledge in inference-based problem solving approaches.
CO3Apply probability theory to describe and model agents operating in uncertain environments.
CO4Implement optimisation models of computation and processing in real-world applications of intelligent agents.
CO5Apply principles of cooperative and competitive multi-agent decision making by constructing conceptual frameworks.
Learning Outcomes
LOOutcome

Course Objectives
COObjective
CO1Introduce students to the data models, storages, and querying languages used in data management, emphasizing machine learning aspects.

CO3Deliver hands-on exposure to the common techniques and tools used by data engineers to build, test, deploy, and automate ML pipelines.

Learning Outcomes
LOOutcome

LO4Apply conceptual data management knowledge over a real-world ML workflow, addressing model metadata, privacy, and monitoring.
